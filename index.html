✨ ESP32 Starter Kit

Learn Electronics!

10 hands-on projects — flash code directly from your browser, no software needed.

✅ Works on Chromebook  |  🌐 Chrome Browser Only  |  🔌 Just plug in your ESP32!

How It Works

Four steps to your first blinking LED

1

🔌 Plug In

Connect ESP32 to Chromebook with the included Micro-USB cable

2

🔧 Wire It

Follow the wiring guide shown on each project card

3

⚡ Flash It

Click Install, pick your port from the popup, done!

4

🎉 Play!

Watch your project come to life and experiment!

💡

1 — Blink

⭐ Beginner

The "Hello World" of electronics! Make the built-in LED blink on and off. Learn the basics of digital outputs and code loops.

🔌 Wiring No wiring needed — uses the built-in LED on GPIO 2
GPIO LED digitalWrite
⚠️ Open in Chrome 🔒 Needs HTTPS
🚦

2 — Traffic Light

⭐ Beginner

Build a real traffic light sequence with red, yellow, and green LEDs! Learn about multiple outputs and timing.

🔌 Wiring Red LED → GPIO 25 + 220Ω resistor → GND
Yellow LED → GPIO 26 + 220Ω → GND
Green LED → GPIO 27 + 220Ω → GND
LEDs Resistors Timing Breadboard
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🔘

3 — Push Button

⭐ Beginner

Press a button to turn an LED on and off. Your first interactive project! Learn digital inputs, pull-up resistors, and reading button states.

🔌 Wiring Button: one leg → GPIO 14, other → GND
LED → GPIO 12 + 220Ω → GND
Button digitalRead Pull-up Input
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🎛️

4 — Dimmer Knob

⭐ Beginner

Turn the potentiometer knob to dim and brighten an LED using PWM. Learn about analog inputs and pulse-width modulation!

🔌 Wiring Pot: Left → GND, Middle → GPIO 34, Right → 3.3V
LED → GPIO 25 + 220Ω → GND
Potentiometer PWM analogRead analogWrite
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🌡️

5 — Weather Station

⭐⭐ Intermediate

Read real temperature and humidity with the DHT11 sensor and display it live on the OLED screen. Your own mini weather station!

🔌 Wiring DHT11: VCC → 3.3V, GND → GND, DATA → GPIO 4
OLED: VCC → 3.3V, GND → GND, SDA → 21, SCL → 22
DHT11 OLED I2C Sensors
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🚨

6 — Motion Alarm

⭐⭐ Intermediate

The PIR sensor detects movement in the room and triggers an LED alarm! Perfect intro to passive infrared sensors and interrupts.

🔌 Wiring PIR: VCC → 5V, GND → GND, OUT → GPIO 13
Red LED → GPIO 12 + 220Ω → GND
PIR Sensor Motion Alarm digitalRead
⚠️ Open in Chrome
☀️

7 — Light Meter

⭐⭐ Intermediate

The photoresistor detects how bright the room is and shows a live bar graph on the OLED. Cover it with your hand to see the values change!

🔌 Wiring Photo resistor: one leg → 3.3V, other → GPIO 34
10KΩ resistor: GPIO 34 → GND (voltage divider)
OLED: SDA → 21, SCL → 22
Photoresistor analogRead OLED Bar Graph
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🤖

8 — Obstacle Detector

⭐⭐ Intermediate

The IR obstacle module detects objects in front of it. Red LED = object detected, green LED = clear path. The building block of every robot!

🔌 Wiring IR Module: VCC → 3.3V, GND → GND, OUT → GPIO 15
Red LED → GPIO 25 + 220Ω → GND
Green LED → GPIO 26 + 220Ω → GND
IR Sensor Obstacle Robotics Digital Input
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🌐

9 — WiFi Controller

⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

The ESP32 creates its own WiFi hotspot and hosts a webpage where you can control LEDs from your phone or Chromebook browser. No internet needed!

🔌 Wiring Red LED → GPIO 25 + 220Ω → GND
Green LED → GPIO 26 + 220Ω → GND
Blue LED → GPIO 27 + 220Ω → GND
Connect to WiFi: ESP32-Kit / pass: 12345678
WiFi Web Server IoT HTML Hotspot
⚠️ Open in Chrome
🛰️

10 — Smart Dashboard

⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

The ultimate project! All sensors combined — temperature, humidity, light, and motion — displayed on the OLED and on a live WiFi webpage dashboard.

🔌 Wiring DHT11 → GPIO 4  |  PIR → GPIO 13
Photo resistor → GPIO 34 + 10KΩ → GND
OLED: SDA → 21, SCL → 22
Connect to WiFi: ESP32-Kit / pass: 12345678
DHT11 PIR Light Sensor OLED WiFi Dashboard
⚠️ Open in Chrome
